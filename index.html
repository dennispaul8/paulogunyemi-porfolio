How a piece gets made

Workflow
01

Concept and prompt design

Start from a brief or idea, then build the prompt structure — style, composition, motion — before generating.

02

Generate and iterate

Run passes across Kling, Higgsfield, and Google Flow, comparing outputs and refining prompts until the result holds up.

03

Upscale and polish

Push selected frames through Magnific for resolution and detail, then final-check for consistency.

04

Publish

Format for the platform and ship — with an eye on what actually performs, not just what looks good in isolation.
